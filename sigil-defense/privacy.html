Sigil Defense: Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026 · Developer: MoeStudio

This policy explains what information the Sigil Defense game ("the app") uses. In short: we do not collect, store, or share any personal information.

Information we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no in-app purchases, and no analytics or tracking added by us. We never see who you are or how you play.

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, the app saves the following only on your own device:

  • which levels you have unlocked and the stars you earned;
  • your sound settings (music and sound-effect volume, mute).

This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes it.

Game engine

Sigil Defense is made with the Unity game engine. Unity may collect limited, anonymous technical information (for example device model and operating-system version) to operate and improve its engine. This does not identify you. See Unity's privacy policy for game players for details.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information, it does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts collecting information (for example if a future update adds online features), this page will be updated before that happens, with a new effective date.
