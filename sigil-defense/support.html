How to play

  • Tap a tower button on the right, then tap a build spot (the dashed squares). Tap the button again to cancel.
  • Tap a built tower to upgrade or sell it.
  • Drag the map sideways to see all of it.
  • Archers shred fast packs, Cannons reach the farthest, Mages break armored enemies and bosses. Mix them.

Progress

Your unlocked levels and stars are saved on your device. Deleting the app resets them.
