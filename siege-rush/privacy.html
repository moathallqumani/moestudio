Siege Rush: Keep Wars: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026 · Developer: Muath Alluqmani (MoeStudio)

This policy explains what information Siege Rush: Keep Wars ("the Game") uses.

Short version

  • The Game is played offline against the computer. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Your progress is saved only on your device. We never receive it.
  • We only get information from you if you choose to send us a problem report by email.

1. Information stored on your device

To save your progress, the Game keeps a file on your device with:

  • your player name and avatar;
  • a random player ID created by the Game (for example 868E-57CC), not linked to your real identity;
  • your army, deck and tower choices, Keep level and XP;
  • match statistics (matches, wins, losses, best streak, Tavern time);
  • your settings (music and sound volume, vibration, language).

This file stays on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the Game deletes it.

2. Problem reports you send us

In Settings → Report a problem you can write a message and tap Send. This opens your own email app with the message ready. Nothing is sent until you send that email yourself. The email contains:

  • the text you wrote;
  • the Game version, your in-game player ID, your device model, operating system and the Game's language;
  • your email address (because it comes from your email account).

We use this only to understand and fix the problem and to reply to you. We do not sell it or share it. We delete report emails when they are no longer needed, or sooner if you ask us.

3. Information we do not collect

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, advertising ID, payment details or any other personal data. The Game does not use cookies.

4. Game engine

The Game is made with the Unity game engine. Unity may collect limited, anonymous technical information (for example device model and operating-system version) to operate and improve its engine. This does not identify you. See Unity's privacy policy for game players for details.

5. App stores

You download the Game from the Apple App Store or Google Play. Apple and Google may collect information under their own privacy policies; we do not control that and we do not receive it.

6. Children

The Game does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. If a child sends us a problem report, we use it only to reply and then delete it. A parent can ask us to delete any message at any time.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to see or delete any email you sent us. Your saved progress is only on your device and you can delete it by uninstalling the Game.

8. Changes

If the Game starts using online play, accounts, analytics or ads, this page will be updated before that version is released, with a new date.
