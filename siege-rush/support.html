Contact

Found a bug, have a question, or an idea? The fastest way is Settings → Report a problem in the game: it adds your game version and device for us. You can also email xnukepro@gmail.com and include your device model.

How to play

  • Tap a card at the bottom to train that unit. It costs gold; units march out on their own.
  • Gold comes from your Gold Mine. Tap the mine to upgrade it, and tap your tower to upgrade it too. The tower can't be a higher level than the mine.
  • Capture the Tavern in the middle by standing units in its ring. While you hold it, your army earns more gold, deals more damage and trains faster.
  • Tap the Tavern to choose HOLD (guards stay) or ATTACK (everyone pushes), or tap a guard to send it to attack.
  • Destroy the enemy castle to win. If time runs out, sudden death starts; then whoever held the Tavern longest wins.
  • New to the game? Tap ? on the home screen for a guided first battle.

Progress

Your army, deck, level and stats are saved on your device. Deleting the app resets them.
